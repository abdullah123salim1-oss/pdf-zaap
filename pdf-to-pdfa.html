Questions & Answers

What is PDF/A and why is it used? ▼

PDF/A is an ISO-standardized version of PDF designed specifically for long-term digital archiving.

Does PDF/A embed all necessary fonts automatically? ▼

Yes, font metrics and color spaces are embedded to guarantee exact visual reproduction across future devices.

Are my archive files uploaded to external web servers? ▼

No, all PDF/A compliance structures are generated locally inside your web browser.

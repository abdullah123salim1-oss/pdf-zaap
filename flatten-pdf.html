Why Use PDFZaap's Flatten PDF?

Prevent unauthorized editing of completed PDF forms, digital signatures, and comments with PDFZaap's Flatten PDF tool. Flattening renders interactive form fields and vector annotations directly into the permanent page content layer, turning fillable documents into static, tamper-proof PDFs. Because this layer consolidation occurs inside your browser, confidential tax forms, application filings, and signed contracts are protected from server exposure. Secure your finalized documents instantly for free with zero watermarks.

Questions & Answers

What does flattening a PDF actually do? ▼

It merges fillable form inputs, signature layers, and comments into the background, making them uneditable.

Can someone un-flatten the PDF later? ▼

Once flattened, form fields and annotations become permanent visual page graphics and cannot be edited.

Are my files uploaded during flattening? ▼

No, PDFZaap flattens page streams entirely inside your local browser sandbox.

Privacy Policy & Cookies

Welcome to PDFZaap. Your privacy is of paramount importance to us. This document details how we handle cookies, privacy, and data transfer inside our SaaS platform.

1. File & Data Security

All file uploads process through secure, transient cache memories protected by 256-bit SSL encryption layer. We enforce a strict zero-retention system: your documents are automatically swept and deleted from our active caches within 60 minutes after execution.

2. Client-Side Integrity

Most basic structural and style configurations run client-side inside your own local browser variables. We do not inspect, retain, or share document elements.

3. Cookie Utilization

We leverage small analytical cookies to analyze network traffic patterns and provide non-intrusive advertisements. Cookies do not log personal user identifiers, email addresses, or account structures.

4. Third-Party Ads

We partner with Google AdSense to deliver relevant advertisement spots. These vendors may use cookies to serve personalized ads based on your visits to this and other sites on the web.

PNGs are the format of choice for screenshots, diagrams and graphics — and they rarely need to travel alone. This tool places your PNG files on PDF pages in the order you add them: each image can fill its own page, or be centered on a standard A4 / US Letter page. Why use PDFZaap to bundle graphics into documents privately

Local and watermark-free. Image-to-PDF sites typically upload your images, cap free usage, or stamp a watermark. This runs in your browser: the PNGs are embedded into the PDF in your tab and the file downloads to your device — no account, no watermark, no usage meter.

PNG strengths, honestly stated. PNG keeps flat colors, text and line art crisp, which makes it ideal for diagrams and UI screenshots. The PDF pages remain raster — no searchable text layer is added. For photos where file size matters more than artifact-free edges, JPG to PDF produces a smaller file at the same workflow.

Tips & common problems

  • Screenshots export perfectly — keep them at native resolution to avoid blur on A4 pages.
  • For transparent PNGs, the PDF page is white behind the graphic; design with that in mind.
  • Large batches: sort your files numerically before selecting, so the page order is the filename order.
  • If the combined PDF is too heavy for an email, run Compress PDF afterwards.
  • Screenshots of code or UI? Keep them at the display's native resolution so text stays sharp on the PDF page.

When to use this vs. related tools

Use this for graphics-heavy documents; JPG to PDF for photos; Merge PDF when you are combining existing PDFs; and Split PDF to break a finished PDF apart. Questions & Answers

Does it support transparency?▼

The image keeps its transparency inside the PDF page, but the page background is white — there is no 'transparent PDF page' concept.

Can I combine many images?▼

Yes, add as many PNGs as you need; each becomes one page.

Is there a file size limit?▼

No hard limit — the browser handles the embedding locally, so your device's memory is the practical ceiling.

Why choose PNG over JPG for this?▼

PNG is lossless for flat graphics and text. For photographs, JPG at good quality is smaller and usually fine — use the JPG tool for those.

Why does my PNG look pixelated on the page?▼

If the source image is small but the page is large, the image simply has fewer pixels than the page needs — capture at higher resolution rather than letting the tool stretch it.

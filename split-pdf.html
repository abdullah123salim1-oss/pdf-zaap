Why Use PDFZaap's Split PDF?

Sometimes you only need one chapter, one invoice page, or one signed form out of a much larger document. PDFZaap lets you type a simple page range — single pages, ranges, or a mix of both — and instantly pulls out exactly those pages into a brand new PDF. The entire operation runs inside your browser's local memory, so the original document never touches an external server. There's no page limit, no watermark on the output, and no charge no matter how many times you split a file.

Questions & Answers

What page ranges are supported? ▼

You can define simple ranges like '1-3' or comma-separated lists like '1,3,5'.

How fast is splitting? ▼

Splitting is processed instantly using your device's local resources.

Will the extracted pages lose quality? ▼

No, pages are copied directly without any re-rendering or compression.

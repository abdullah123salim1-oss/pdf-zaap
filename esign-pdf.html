Streamline legal agreements, employment offers, and client invoices with PDFZaap's E-Sign PDF tool. Questions & Answers

Can I draw or upload my own signature? ▼

Yes, PDFZaap allows drawing, typing, or uploading image files of your signature.

Are my signed documents legally binding? ▼

Electronic signatures applied via browser canvas are accepted for most standard agreement workflows.

Is my signature saved on a server? ▼

No, your signature is embedded locally into the document and never stored on any server.

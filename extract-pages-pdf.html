Questions & Answers

How do I select which pages to extract? ▼

Click on page thumbnails or enter page numbers in the input box to choose which pages to extract.

Does extracting pages affect original quality? ▼

No, extracted pages retain 100% of their original text formatting, vector graphics, and image quality.

Are my files kept private during extraction? ▼

Yes, all extraction functions execute locally in your web browser with zero server uploads.

How to Sign a PDF on iPhone Without an App (Free & Easy)

Published September 19, 2026 · 7 min read · By Abdullah Salim

Someone emails you a contract, rental agreement, or parental consent slip that urgently needs your signature. You are away from your home desk, nowhere near a printer or flatbed scanner, and holding only your iPhone. Downloading bloated App Store apps that trap you in recurring $9.99/week subscriptions or force you through mandatory account creation is unnecessary.

Your iPhone already contains the hardware and software required to produce legally binding electronic signatures. Here are the two fastest ways to sign any PDF on iOS without installing any apps.

Quick Answer:

You can sign a PDF on iPhone natively using Apple's Markup feature: open the PDF in the Files app or Mail, tap the pencil Markup icon, tap + > Signature, draw your signature with your finger, and place it. Method 2: Using Built-in Apple Markup (iOS Native)

If your PDF is attached directly to an Apple Mail message or stored in the native Files app, Apple's built-in Markup toolkit lets you sign without launching a browser.

Step 1: Open the PDF in Files or Mail

Tap the document in the Files app to preview it full-screen.

Step 2: Tap the Markup Icon

Look for the circular pen icon in the top-right corner of the navigation bar. Tap it to reveal Apple's drawing and annotation toolbar at the bottom of the screen.

Step 3: Tap the "+" Button and Choose "Signature"

In the bottom toolbar, tap the + (Plus) icon in the lower-right corner. In the menu that appears, tap Signature (or Add or Remove Signature if you have previously saved one).

Step 4: Sign with Your Finger

A blank signing canvas will appear in landscape orientation. Trace your signature with your fingertip. When happy with the result, tap Done at the top right.

Step 5: Drag and Resize onto the Line

The signature drops onto the center of the PDF page with a blue bounding box. Drag it over the dotted signature line. Drag the blue corner dots inward or outward to scale it appropriately. Tap Done in the top-left corner to save the changes.

Are Mobile Signatures Legally Enforceable?

One of the most frequent concerns when signing contracts on a smartphone screen is legal admissibility. In virtually all developed legal jurisdictions, electronic signatures carry identical enforceability to ink signatures:

  • United States: The federal Electronic Signatures in Global and National Commerce Act (ESIGN) and the Uniform Electronic Transactions Act (UETA) establish that a contract or signature cannot be denied legal effect solely because it is electronic.
  • United Kingdom: The Electronic Communications Act 2000 and UK eIDAS recognize electronic signatures as valid and admissible in court.
  • European Union: The eIDAS regulation (EU No 910/2014) confirms that electronic signatures cannot be dismissed purely due to their electronic nature.

Whether you draw your signature on an iPhone screen or stamp it via software, what matters is your clear intent to sign and the document’s subsequent integrity.

Pro Tips for Flawless iPhone PDF Signing

1. Turn Your iPhone to Landscape for Complex Signatures

Signing in portrait mode restricts finger travel and can result in cramped, messy signatures. Turning your phone to landscape mode expands the signing canvas, giving your finger enough room to make natural flourishes.

2. Compress the Signed File Before Emailing

Annotating and signing a PDF on iOS sometimes inflates file size because the device embeds high-resolution raster layers for the pen strokes. Pass your signed document through PDFZaap Compress PDF so it flies through email filters.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is an electronic signature made on an iPhone legally binding?

Yes. Under the US Federal ESIGN Act, the Uniform Electronic Transactions Act (UETA), and European eIDAS regulations, electronic signatures carried out on mobile touchscreens carry equal legal validity to pen-and-paper signatures for most business agreements, leases, NDAs, and consumer purchases.

How do I sign a PDF received in an email on my iPhone?

Open the email attachment in Apple Mail, tap the pencil icon (Markup) in the top-right corner, tap the '+' button at the bottom, select 'Signature', draw your name, and position it on the signature line. Tap Done to reply directly with the signed document.

Can anyone copy my signature out of a signed PDF?

If a PDF is signed using loose annotation layers, someone could theoretically copy the image layer. To prevent this, use PDFZaap's Flatten PDF tool after signing. Flattening permanently burns the signature image into the static page canvas, preventing anyone from isolating or extracting the signature graphic.

Do I need to pay for Adobe Sign or DocuSign to sign a simple document?

No. While platforms like DocuSign cater to enterprise audit trail workflows, signing personal agreements, job offers, invoices, and permission slips requires no paid subscription. Both iOS Markup and PDFZaap's eSign tool are 100% free with no monthly limits.

Can I add the date and my printed name on my iPhone too?

Yes. In Apple Markup, tap the '+' icon and choose 'Text' to type your name or the date. In PDFZaap's eSign tool, you can click the Text option to insert neat, aligned typography directly beside your signature.

Why Do Merged PDFs Always Have Broken Numbering?

When you use a merge tool to combine several documents (for example, combining Chapter 1, Chapter 2, and an Appendix), each individual file preserves its original pagination. As a result, page 1 occurs three separate times throughout the document, confusing readers and frustrating referees.

To fix this, you must apply a fresh pagination overlay across the unified document container. This stamps a continuous sequence (e.g., Pages 1 through 84) regardless of how many original documents were joined together.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I skip numbering the title cover page or table of contents?

Yes. PDFZaap's Number PDF Pages tool allows you to specify a starting page number and start-from offset. For example, you can begin visual numbering on page 2 or page 3 while leaving your cover page clean.

Can I add 'Page X of Y' pagination across my entire document?

Yes. You can choose standard numbering (1, 2, 3), dash notation (- 1 -), or comprehensive 'Page X of Y' formats, which automatically counts the total page count across your document.

Does macOS Preview have an automated tool to add page numbers?

No. Apple Preview lacks an automatic batch page numbering engine. You would be forced to create and manually align individual text annotation boxes on every single page. Using PDFZaap in Safari numbers a 100-page document in two seconds.

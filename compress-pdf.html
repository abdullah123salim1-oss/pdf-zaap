Why Use PDFZaap's Compress PDF?

Large PDF files can clog email attachments and slow down uploads to job portals or client systems. PDFZaap's compressor re-renders each page locally and lets you control the exact image quality trade-off with a simple slider, so you decide how aggressively to shrink your file. Text stays sharp and legible even at higher compression levels, since the tool intelligently balances image detail against file size. And because every calculation happens on your own device, your compressed document never passes through an external server.

Questions & Answers

What does the quality slider change? ▼

The slider adjusts the embedded image resolution and quality — lower values mean smaller files but softer images.

Will compressing affect the text? ▼

Text remains sharp and selectable; compression primarily affects embedded images.

Is there a file size limit? ▼

No, PDFZaap compresses locally so there is no artificial file size cap.

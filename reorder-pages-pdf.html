Questions & Answers

How do I reorder pages? ▼

Upload your PDF, then drag and drop the visual page thumbnails into your desired order before clicking save.

Can I delete unwanted pages while reordering? ▼

Yes, you can delete individual pages directly from the thumbnail view before generating the final file.

Is my document uploaded to a server? ▼

No, page reordering is processed locally in your browser for total privacy.

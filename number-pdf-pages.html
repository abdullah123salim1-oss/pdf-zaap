Questions & Answers

Where can I place page numbers? ▼

You can place page numbers at the top (header) or bottom (footer) in left, center, or right alignment.

Can I format the page numbers? ▼

Yes, choose formats like "1", "Page 1", or "1 of N" along with custom font size options.

Are my files uploaded to any server? ▼

No, all page numbering is performed locally in your web browser sandbox.
